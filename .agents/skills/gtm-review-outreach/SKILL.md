# Review a proposed message

Identify the supplied sender, audience, relationship stage, channel, offer and evidence. Respect the operator’s wording for a specific campaign. Flag any conflict with a frozen role packet.

Read the text as the person receiving it. What would they understand about the sender and the offer? What effort would replying require? A published business channel can support a draft addressed to reception. Its existence establishes neither a buying need nor permission to send.

Apply the six dimensions in the [writing and review policy](../../../gtm_agents/outreach.py). Quote the phrase behind a material failure and record a concise pass, revise or unknown judgment. For cold WhatsApp, look for a relevant opening, a brief offer and one easy invitation to continue. For a warm response, answer the actual request.

When reviewing a Sales submission, assess the submitted version. Record any suggested replacement separately so the review remains attributable. Withhold copy if a material claim has no support. Keep editorial quality, destination verification and permission to contact as separate decisions.
